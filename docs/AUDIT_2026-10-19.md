# BiG Equity 360: functional audit (2026-10-19)

This was a read-only audit. No code, data or configuration was changed, and nothing that creates state (theses, watchlists, alerts, studies, backtests, scenarios) was called.

**Environment:**
- API on :8010, serving snapshot `R20261019T212443TSXF`, built 2026-10-19 21:39 UTC.
- Its `serving_as_of` is **2026-10-13**, not 2026-10-16.
- Dashboard on localhost:5173.

**Method:**
- **API sweep:** 391 sequential calls covering 138 of the 148 GET operations. The 10 not covered have no instances to call: no theses, peer sets or saved strategies exist. Two read-only POST compute calls were also made: scenario run and supply-chain shock.
- **UI sweep:** one headless Chrome, 44 routes plus every tab trigger, dark at 1440 px and light at 1280 px (170 page/tab loads). The Markets Overview was also run with the region set to EU, CN/HK and KR.
- **Data checks:** DuckDB read-only queries on the serving snapshot, and lazy Polars scans of the lake and `job_runs`.
- Screenshots are in the session scratchpad (`scratchpad/audit/ui/`), not in the repo.

## 1. Executive summary

1. **The nightly pipeline is not running, and nothing in the UI says so (P0).**
   - Every worker started since 2026-10-10 was launched with `--no-schedule`. The current one is pid 2020, started 19-10 22:40 with `python -m app.scheduler.worker --no-schedule`.
   - `job_runs` contains **zero** runs with trigger `schedule`. No worker ran at all between 10-13 06:18 and 10-19 18:49.
   - As a result, prices stop at **10-13**: 10-14, 10-15 and 10-16 are missing in every region, and US 10-13 has only 68 bars.
   - Macro, filings, insider, GDELT and analytics tables all stop at 10-11 to 10-13.
   - The weekly DAG (due 10-17) never ran.
   - The header still reads "As of Oct 13 · 27m ago". That relative time is the serving *rebuild* time, so the app looks fresh.
2. **Price-history integrity has a few high-impact defects.**
   - **Ticker reuse is stitched into one series:**
     - SPCX: SpaceX's listing sits on the old SPCX ETF history, giving +650% YTD at a $958bn market cap.
     - BNY: shows +1,413% YTD on top of an old $10 muni-fund history.
     - FIG: shows the same pattern.
   - SPCX alone drives US Industrials to **+96% YTD** in the sector heatmap.
   - A full trading day (**2026-10-12**) is missing for EU, UK, HK and KR, while the markets were open.
   - Several 100× unit flips remain, for example ROSE.L and DIA.MC.
3. **Macro > Markets > Overview is the weakest page in the app. The user's suspicion is right.**
   - It ignores half of its own payload: the API returns indices, breadth, gainers and losers, and the UI renders none of them.
   - Several labels are wrong:
     - "z" on raw levels (Growth 100.88 z, Vol 14.92 z);
     - "1M chg" on a one-day change;
     - "5y" on a 60-observation (about 3-month) sparkline;
     - "10y pct" on a normal-CDF of a 5-year z-score.
   - Macro tiles show CPI and GDP as index levels (334.1, 24,408).
   - For EU, JP and CN/HK it shows "Expansion · **0%** posterior probability", read from a `pending` stub. For KR and LATAM it silently shows the **US** regime.
   - The cockpit (`/macro`) already does almost everything better. Section 4 has a redesign.
4. **The UI is technically clean.**
   - There were 0 failed network requests and 0 page errors in 170 loads, and no page-level horizontal overflow at 1440 or 1280 px.
   - The only console errors are 2 duplicate-React-key errors, on the Company 360 Model tab.
   - The median load to network-quiet was 2.7 s and the maximum 5.8 s (ECB page).
   - The API is fast: 57 ms median and 307 ms p95. Three payloads are over 1.9 MB.
   - Most remaining problems are about content honesty and freshness, not crashes.
5. **Reliability gaps would stop unattended running.**
   - The worker depends on an open PowerShell window, has no OS-level scheduler or service, and has no "scheduler off" alarm.
   - A single analytics failure blocks the whole serving rebuild, including fresh prices.
   - Timed-out thread jobs keep running as zombies.
   - `ref_universe`, which all price jobs hard-depend on, has never succeeded: 3 failed runs, the last one partial.
   - 34 of 157 registered jobs have never been executed by the runner.

**Counts:** 2 P0, 16 P1, 16 P2, 8 P3 (42 findings), plus 10 reliability notes (R1–R10) in §6.

## 4. Deep dive: Macro › Markets › Overview (`/macro/markets`, default tab)

### 4.2 What a portfolio manager needs from a "Markets overview"

On the first screen, a PM should be able to answer: what moved, how broad was the move, is risk on or off, what are rates, credit and the dollar doing, and is any of this stale.

The current page answers none of these at a glance:
- no index levels or returns;
- no breadth;
- no FX, commodities or volatility;
- no risk-on/off reading, even though the Cross-asset page already computes a composite;
- no as-of dates.

It also partly duplicates the cockpit, which has the better macro heatmap, cycle clock and recession panel.

### 4.3 Redesign proposal (data already exists for nearly all of it)

1. **Header strip.**
   - Show "Market data as of {date per region}" and a stale badge per region (F06).
   - Show a risk-on/off gauge from `/cross-asset/risk` (+0.08 neutral today), with a link to Cross-asset.
2. **Row 1: index board** (`indices`, already returned).
   - 12 benchmarks with level, 1D/1W/1M/YTD and a 60-day spark.
   - Local currency plus a USD toggle (FX exists).
   - Sort by region; highlight moves greater than 2σ.
3. **Row 2: cross-asset tape**, from `/cross-asset/cockpit`, which already has trend, carry and vol z-scores.
   - Rates: UST 2Y/10Y, Bund 10Y, JGB 10Y, with level and 1D/1M change in bp.
   - FX: DXY, EURUSD, USDJPY, USDCNH.
   - Commodities: WTI/Brent, gold, copper.
   - Vol: VIX, MOVE if available.

## 6. Reliability findings (unattended operation)

| # | Finding | Evidence | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Scheduling depends on an interactive window and a flag that has been left off | F01. `run_worker.ps1` / `run_all.ps1` open PowerShell windows; nothing registers a Windows Scheduled Task or service. No sleep prevention (no `SetThreadExecutionState` / `powercfg`). APScheduler `misfire_grace_time` is 6 h, so a laptop asleep through 01:30 + 6 h skips the night. | Register a Scheduled Task at logon ("run whether user is logged on", restart on failure), or run the nightly from Task Scheduler directly with "wake the computer to run this task". Call `SetThreadExecutionState(ES_SYSTEM_REQUIRED)` while a DAG runs. Alert when the last scheduled nightly is more than 26 h old. |
